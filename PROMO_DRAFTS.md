## X / Twitter drafts (all under 280 chars)

**1. The announcement**
I built Snag: the first frictionless way to give with crypto. No separate donation, no new token to buy — when you send ETH or BTC, opt in and 1% goes to a cause you pick. Every payment you already make quietly funds something good.

**2. The "no new token" angle**
Charity tokens make you buy their coin before you can give. Snag doesn't. It works on the ETH and BTC you already hold — 1% of any send, routed to a cause you choose. Giving shouldn't require joining a new ecosystem.

**3. The "no platform cut" angle**
Most crypto donation platforms take ~2.5% off the top. Snag takes nothing — 100% of your 1% reaches the cause wallet you picked. Opt-in, on-chain, verifiable. No middleman, no black box.

**4. The specific-causes angle**
Not a vague charity fund — a wallet. "Utility Bill Help for Single Mothers" is an actual address your 1% lands in. 8 specific causes on ETH + BTC, every snag verifiable on-chain. You pick exactly where it goes.

**5. The invitation**
Snag is simple: send crypto like normal, opt in, and 1% goes to the cause you choose. No extra step, no new token, no platform cut. Try the demo and tell me what you'd change — honest feedback wanted.

## LinkedIn-style post

I want to share something I've been building: Snag — the first frictionless way to give with crypto.

I'll be upfront: charity tokens and crypto donation platforms already exist. What's been missing is a way to give that asks nothing extra of you. Snag puts the gift inside payments you already make. When you send ETH or BTC, you opt in once, and 1% of the send is automatically routed to a charitable cause you choose — things like diapers for low-income families, utility bill help for single mothers, groceries, school supplies, medical bill relief, shelter, clean water, and disaster relief.

Three things I insisted on:

- **No separate donation step.** The send *is* the donation. The gift happens inside the on-chain transaction itself.
- **No new token to buy or hold.** Snag works on the native ETH and BTC you already have — no new ecosystem to join.
- **No platform cut.** Processors typically charge ~2.5%. Snag takes nothing: 100% of the 1% reaches the cause wallet you picked, verifiable on-chain.

I believe small, repeated acts of giving add up — especially when giving is built into something people already do every day.

If you're curious, I'd love your honest feedback. And if you work with a charity or nonprofit, I'd love to talk about getting your cause listed.

— Divine
